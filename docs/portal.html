How this is priced
  • The forward rate is the Garman-Kohlhagen forward for USD/CAD: spot times e^((CAD rate - USD rate) x tenor). It is set at the market, so the trade starts worth about zero.
  • The credit charge is CVA: the bank's expected loss if you default while the trade is in its favour, from simulated exchange-rate paths, with independent defaults and no wrong-way risk. It is shown as a running spread in basis points a year, and as the equivalent upfront amount.
  • A CSA lowers it because collateral covers most of the exposure; only the margin period (10 business days) stays uncovered.
  • Flat rates and constant volatility. The credit line is checked against the 95th percentile peak exposure of your whole netting set (this trade plus one small existing forward).
  • Every number is looked up from a grid produced by bench/bench_portal.py calling POST /risk/predeal; the page does no pricing.
