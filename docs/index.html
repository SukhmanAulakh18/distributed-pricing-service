Four terms, in plain English

FX forward
A contract to exchange currency later at a rate fixed today, so a company knows what its USD will be worth.
CVA
Credit valuation adjustment: what the bank charges for the risk that the client defaults while the trade is open.
PFE
Potential future exposure: how much the client could owe the bank in a bad case, the 95th percentile of simulated outcomes.
CSA
Credit support annex: a collateral agreement where both sides post cash as the trade moves, which shrinks exposure and the charge.

How a request becomes a decision

  1. A request arrives

    The API takes a pre-deal check or a book of trades.

  2. Work fans out

    Redis Streams feeds workers that KEDA scales.

  3. C++ does the maths

    The same scenarios price the book before and after the trade.

  4. A decision comes back

    A rate, a charge, a verdict. Cassandra keeps the record.
